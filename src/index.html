SLIPSTREAM/SETTINGS

AGraphics

Rendering runs on the GPU through WebGL 2 → Metal. "Performance" renders at 1× pixel density with 2× MSAA; "Ultra" renders at full Retina density with 4× MSAA and 4K shadows.

BFlight & Controls

Flight assist ON: every aircraft uses a rate-command flight computer (the surfaces still move the air — the computer just moves them for you). OFF: piston aircraft get raw stick-to-surface linkage, so you can stall, spin and fight torque yourself. Jets with real fly-by-wire keep it.
